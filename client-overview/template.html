01 · The idea

Estimating you can trust, built on your own numbers.

{{PRODUCT_NAME}} is an estimating platform for heavy civil and sitework contractors. It drafts the slowest, most error-prone parts of a bid (reading the plan set, pulling quantities, building material lists, chasing supplier prices). Your estimator then confirms every number. The result is an estimate built from your labor, equipment, production rates, and supplier quotes. Not industry averages.

1

Your numbers, not ours

{{PRODUCT_NAME}} ships empty by design. No built-in rates, no seeded price database, no “typical” production. Every figure in an estimate is entered, uploaded, or confirmed by your team.

2

AI assists. You verify.

AI drafts quantities, materials, and prices. Nothing reaches an estimate until an estimator confirms it, and every AI-suggested value is labeled as such until then.

3

Every number is traceable

Each quantity links to the document, sheet, and location it came from. Each price shows its source and date. Any line can explain its own math.

What your team gets

One home for your costsLabor, equipment, materials, suppliers, and production rates, refined anytime.
A reviewed takeoffDrafted from the owner’s own bid schedule and plan tables, confirmed by you.
Supplier-ready RFQsNumbered, branded, and grouped by supplier category, with quotes compared side by side.
Bids that match the formEstimates organized by the owner’s bid items, exported in their exact order.

Who it is built for

Estimators bidding earthwork, utilities, paving, and structures (bridges) on federal, state DOT, county/city, and private work. Here, precision beats speed. If the numbers don’t match reality, the tool doesn’t get used, so every design decision starts with accuracy.

Fits how you already work

One bid, start to finish.

03 · Capabilities

What we are building.

Six connected modules. Your team can start with what it has and improve over time. Nothing is locked, everything autosaves, and every screen explains itself in plain language.

1

Company Setup: “Make It Yours”

Set your costs once. Refine them anytime. Always one click away in the main menu.

Company basics

Logo, accent color, markup, overhead, sales tax, project types bid, bonding and insurance approach, and your standard RFQ email.

Labor

Roles with base wage, burden %, and a separate prevailing / Davis-Bacon rate. Or upload a wage determination and review the extraction.

Equipment

Ownership, operating, and standby cost per hour plus mobilization per move. Or upload an equipment cost report and review the extraction.

Materials & supplier directory

Unit cost, density, waste %, and quote-expiry warnings. Supplier and subcontractor directories by category and service area, with Excel/CSV import.

Production rates

Answer in plain language (“how many CY can your crew place and compact in 8 hours?”) and pick the crew. We handle the formulas.

Soil & material behavior

Your own swell and shrink percentages by soil type, with a before/after volume visual.

Assemblies

Reusable work packages (say, “8-inch sewer, 8 ft deep”) tying materials, crew, equipment, and production together, with a live cost per unit.

Email sending Optional

Send RFQs from {{PRODUCT_NAME}}, your company domain, or each estimator’s own Google or Microsoft mailbox.

Every step is skippable, saveable, and re-editable. A completeness score shows what is left, and estimates flag any line that relies on an unverified value.

2

Plan Room & Takeoff: “Upload the Bid Package”

From a 500-sheet plan set to a reviewed quantity takeoff, with every number traceable.

Upload the whole package

Plan sets of hundreds of sheets and hundreds of MB, bid forms, specs, geotech, addenda, and CAD files (LandXML for earthwork; DXF/DWG stored for reference). Uploads resume if interrupted, and processing runs in the background.

Sheet index

Sheets are split out, title blocks read into a searchable index with thumbnails, and each sheet classified (grading, utility plan & profile, paving, structures, traffic control, and more). You can correct any of it.

Cost control on AI processing

Heavy AI analysis runs only on takeoff-relevant sheets. Before it starts you see the estimated time and cost, your Admin sets limits, and AI cost is logged.

Manual takeoff tools, always available

Plan viewer with per-sheet scale calibration (click two points of known distance) plus linear, polyline, area, and count tools. Click any quantity to jump to its exact spot on the sheet.

Quantities come from three sources, ranked by reliability

1 · Most reliableThe owner’s bid schedule. Extracted into an editable table (item number, description, spec section, unit, quantity). The estimate is organized by these bid items, so your final bid maps 1:1 to the owner’s bid form.
2 · ReliableTabulated data on the plans. Quantity summaries, pipe schedules, structure and manhole tables, curb and sidewalk tables, and earthwork summaries, each with its sheet reference.
3 · Least reliableAI-assisted measurement, always flagged amber. Suggested pipe run lengths, structure counts, and paved areas shown as overlays on the sheet, each with Accept / Edit / Reject.

Earthwork, handled honestly

{{PRODUCT_NAME}} does not guess cut and fill from PDF contours. Earthwork comes from the bid schedule, from comparing imported LandXML surfaces, or from manual entry with a source note, then applies your swell and shrink factors.

Mandatory review

One table lists every extracted or suggested quantity with its source, confidence, and sheet link. Each must be confirmed before it feeds the estimate. Gaps versus the owner’s quantities beyond a % you set are flagged.

Addenda

Upload anytime for a before/after diff of changed items, quantities, and sheets, with version history and acknowledgment tracking. RFQs already sent are flagged, with a revision offered.

Specifications

Requirements referenced by bid items (gradation, binder grade, pipe class, concrete strength) are pulled with section references so RFQs state exactly what is needed. Missing standard specs are flagged.

3

Estimate Builder

Confirmed quantities become an estimate you can explain line by line.

Pre-populated from your takeoff

Organized by the owner’s bid items, or by default sections (Earthwork, Wet Utilities, Paving & Concrete, Structures, Indirect Costs) when there is no bid schedule.

Bid item mapping

Each bid item maps to one or more of your assemblies. AI suggests, you confirm. Unmapped items block final output, so nothing slips through unpriced.

Job-level overrides

Change any rate, production rate, price, or equipment cost for this job without touching company defaults. Overrides are marked and logged with an optional reason.

“How was this calculated?”

On every line: the math in plain English, the values that fed it, and a link to the source sheet.

Confidence indicator

Each line shows if it rests on calibrated data, verified values, supplier quotes, or unverified values. A summary reads, for example, “82% of this estimate is based on calibrated, verified, or quoted data.”

Built for real jobs

Open shop or prevailing wage, haul distances, tax, and permits. Estimates are saved, duplicable, and versioned, and keep their original values unless you recalculate.

4

Material List & Supplier RFQs: “Get Prices”

From confirmed quantities to comparable supplier quotes, however you prefer to send.

Material list

Confirmed quantities × assembly components, using your own conversions (density, thickness, swell and shrink). Identical materials are consolidated across bid items with traceability kept. Fully editable, and subcontractor scope packages work the same way.

Professional RFQ spreadsheets

One per supplier category plus a combined master, each with a unique number (for example IR-2026-0142-AGG-R0) in the file name, header, and email subject. Your logo leads, and only the supplier’s cells are editable.

Two first-class ways to send Your choice

Send from {{PRODUCT_NAME}}: you approve every email; suppliers get the spreadsheet plus a secure no-login form; status and reminders are tracked. Or export and send it yourself: Excel, PDF, CSV, or .zip, with ready-to-paste email text.

Four ways for prices to come back

Online form; filled Excel (auto-matched by RFQ number and line IDs); the supplier’s own PDF quote; or pasted email text. The last two use AI extraction with mandatory review. Bulk upload supported.

Side-by-side quote comparison

Lowest complete price highlighted; missing lines, exclusions, expired quotes, off-spec alternates, and outdated revisions flagged. Selected prices flow into the estimate with supplier, date, and expiry.

Never lose track

Every download and send is logged. If quantities change after an RFQ goes out, a banner offers Revision 1, 2, and so on. Reminders on RFQs you sent yourself come to you.

5

Learn From Past Jobs & Close-Out

Your history becomes the calibration that makes the next estimate sharper.

Teach {{PRODUCT_NAME}} from completed jobs

Upload past estimates and job cost reports (PDF, Excel/CSV, even photos of printed reports). AI extracts the lines; you review them beside the source and map them to your setup. Nothing is auto-imported. Manual entry is available too.

Variance calibration

Estimated versus actual per activity, with suggested adjustments. Each needs your Approve, Edit, or Dismiss. Nothing adjusts on its own.

Close out this job

Enter or upload final actual costs against the saved estimate and feed the same review and calibration, so learning becomes a habit, not a project.

6

Bid Output

Branded documents your team can hand off, and a bid form that matches the owner’s.

Bid summary PDF

Line items, subtotals, markup, and total in your branding (“Powered by {{PRODUCT_NAME}}” small in the footer). Choose a client version that hides internal cost detail, or an internal version.

Bid form export

Unit prices and extensions in the exact item order of the owner’s bid schedule, as Excel and PDF, ready to transfer to the official form.

Full estimate export

The complete estimate in Excel, plus RFQ and quote-comparison exports.

Yours to download

Every file the app generates is downloadable. You are never forced to send, share, or finish anything inside {{PRODUCT_NAME}}.

04 · Built for trust

Built so your estimators will rely on it.

Estimating tools fail when people stop trusting the numbers. Every design decision below keeps your estimator in control and able to explain any figure to a bonding company, an owner, or a boss.

Will it make up numbers?No rates or prices come pre-loaded. Every value is entered, uploaded, or confirmed by your company, and estimates flag exactly which lines rely on unverified values, even if setup isn’t finished.
What if the AI misreads a sheet?AI output is always a draft. Each quantity shows its source, its confidence (amber means low), and a link to the exact spot on the sheet. Nothing feeds the estimate until a person confirms it.
Can we audit any number?Every line has a “How was this calculated?” view in plain English, every price shows its source and date, and bids export in the owner’s exact bid-form order.
Will it overwrite our rates?No. Saved estimates keep their original values unless you explicitly recalculate. Calibration suggestions from past jobs need your Approve, Edit, or Dismiss.
Do we have to change how we email suppliers?No. Send from the platform, or download the files and send from your own inbox to anyone, including people outside your directory. Quotes import the same either way.
Is our data kept separate?Yes. Each company’s records, uploaded files, and quote links are walled off from every other company. That separation is enforced in the database and in file storage, not just on screen, and automated tests attempt cross-company access to records, files, and quote links to confirm it fails.

What {{PRODUCT_NAME}} will deliberately not do

  • Ship with generic production rates, “industry average” costs, or a price database.
  • Automatically import, adjust, or apply anything without your review. Nothing flows into an estimate silently.
  • Send an RFQ you have not previewed and approved.
  • Calculate earthwork from PDF contours, or treat AI measurement as final. It is always flagged for review.
  • Force you to finish a step inside the platform. Every file is downloadable.

How we will know it is working

At kickoff we agree on a few plain measures against your baselines. Examples:

  • Draft quantities agree with your own finished takeoff within a tolerance you set.
  • Estimator time per bid, compared with your baseline.
  • The share of each estimate backed by verified, calibrated, or quoted data (which {{PRODUCT_NAME}} reports on every estimate).
